# Sheba Investments

**A Trading & Investment Ecosystem** — proposed positioning for an emerging trading and investment company.

Sheba is being developed around proprietary trading operations, an independent trader network, market research, brokerage partnerships and a longer-term investment ambition.

The first software release is a premium corporate website and secure lead-generation system. It is not a broker, trading terminal, investment portal or signal execution platform.

## Start here
Read the [documentation index](./docs/README.md), then the product overview, website requirements, technical architecture, data/security, compliance, roadmap and design system.

## Current status
The repository is at the documentation/foundation stage. These documents describe intended direction; they do not mean all business lines or services are operational.

**Initial delivery:** public corporate website, accurate business information, audience-specific enquiry journeys and protected enquiry administration.

**Future work:** internal proprietary trading operations, independent trader portal, approved research/signal distribution, broker referral workflows and investment administration. Each needs separate requirements, approvals and risk review.

## Development rules
- Do not invent licences, clients, traders, partners, performance, capital or operating history.
- Do not claim a service is live when it is planned.
- Do not build trading execution, copy trading, custody, deposits, withdrawals or broker integrations in the first release.
- Keep proprietary trading separate from the independent trader network.
- Read compliance and risk boundaries before implementing financial-services functionality.
- Never commit secrets or production personal data.
