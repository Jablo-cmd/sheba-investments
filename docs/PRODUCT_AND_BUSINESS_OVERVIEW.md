# Sheba Investments — Product and Business Overview

## Vision and positioning
Sheba Investments is being developed as a trading and investment company with the long-term ambition to build complementary business lines around financial markets, professional trading operations, market intelligence and strategic investments.

This is broader than a retail forex signals website. Sheba should develop into an organised business with disciplined processes, clear separation of responsibilities, measurable risk controls and transparent commercial relationships.

**Proposed positioning:** An emerging trading and investment group focused on proprietary trading, market intelligence, trader development and strategic brokerage partnerships.

**Proposed brand line:** “A Trading & Investment Ecosystem.”

These are proposed brand statements, not evidence of scale, authorisation, assets under management or operating history.

## Business lines

### Sheba Proprietary Trading
Purpose: operate company-controlled proprietary trading activity through eligible prop-firm programmes and potentially company capital in the future.

Sheba may evaluate programmes, acquire eligible evaluation/account arrangements, engage professional traders where provider rules expressly permit the intended employee/contractor/team model, establish risk controls, monitor performance and reconcile contractual rewards.

**Critical:** before a trader receives access, retain confirmation that the provider permits the exact arrangement. Do not assume credential sharing, third-party account management, copying or delegation is allowed. If not permitted, do not implement it.

A prop firm's advertised account size or simulated buying power is not automatically capital owned by Sheba. Describe arrangements according to the provider contract.

### Sheba Trading Network
Purpose: build a network of independent traders who may access Sheba-published research or other services after legal and operational approval.

Traders retain control of their own brokerage accounts, make their own decisions and remain responsible for their risk. Sheba may provide commentary, research or signals only after legal classification, permissions, disclosures and controls have been assessed. Broker introductions require transparent, approved commercial arrangements. The initial website does not custody trader funds or collect account credentials.

A long-term aspiration discussed for the network is approximately 10,000 referred traders. This is an ambition only, not a current user count, forecast or achieved metric.

### Sheba Research & Signals
Purpose: develop disciplined research and market intelligence for internal use and, if legally permitted, external services.

If a signal service is approved, every record should contain a unique identifier, instrument, direction, setup, entry zone, stop, targets, risk/reward assumptions, publication timestamp/timezone, invalidation and expiry conditions, updates, author attribution, outcome and complete edit history.

Record losses, cancellations, expired and missed entries as well as wins. Never silently edit or delete losing signals. Disclose methodology, sample period, costs, slippage assumptions and limitations. Internal research and external distribution are different use cases; external distribution needs separate legal and product review.

### Sheba Brokerage Partnerships
Purpose: establish commercial relationships with suitable brokers/platforms, potentially including introducing or referral arrangements.

Potential activities, subject to legal review and contracts: provider due diligence, negotiation, disclosure of commissions/rebates, referral reconciliation and complaint escalation. Do not encourage overtrading or present commercially influenced recommendations as independent.

### Sheba Capital & Investments
A future business line may evaluate longer-term investments or strategic business interests, such as listed securities, ETFs, bonds, private businesses or acquisitions. These are future possibilities, not current offerings. This division needs its own mandate, capital policy, governance, valuation approach, risk limits and legal review.

## Two distinct trader populations

| Dimension | Proprietary trading team | Independent trader network |
|---|---|---|
| Relationship | Employee/contractor subject to contract | Independent user/client/lead |
| Account | Provider arrangement approved for Sheba's model | Trader's own brokerage account |
| Decisions | Within Sheba mandate and controls | Trader retains decision-making |
| Risk | Internal policy and provider rules | Service disclosures; no assumed control of personal account |
| Data | Restricted internal records | Own permitted member records |
| Revenue | Contractual rewards/income where applicable | Approved service fees and/or disclosed partner commissions |

Never mix contracts, permissions, performance records or dashboards between these populations.

## Audiences and revenue concepts
Audiences: independent traders; professional trader candidates; brokers/platforms; strategic partners/investors; general visitors.

Potential revenue, subject to agreements, legal review and readiness: eligible proprietary trading rewards; approved research/service fees; disclosed broker referral commissions; future investment returns. Do not publish illustrative revenue calculations as forecasts or describe revenue as active before agreements, permissions and capability are confirmed.

## Operating principles and claims
Capital preservation and risk discipline; transparency about incentives; no guaranteed returns; no fabricated track records, balances, counts or partner logos; clear business-line separation; documented approvals and audit trails; data minimisation.

Do not portray Sheba as a licensed institution, broker, custodian, exchange, payment institution or discretionary manager unless status and permissions are verified. Do not claim guaranteed profits, a verified track record, an established 10,000-member network, existing partnerships or ownership of prop-firm notional capital without evidence.

Initial product: corporate website, accurate narrative, enquiry forms and secure administration. Later: internal trading operations, approved research, trader portal and referral tracking. Longer term: investment administration after dedicated requirements and approvals.
