# Sheba Investments — Design System and Visual Direction

## Objective
Create a premium, restrained and institutionally credible identity. The website should feel like a serious emerging trading and investment company, not a high-pressure retail forex signal page. Communicate clarity, discipline and trust through typography, spacing, layout and precise language.

## Proposed palette
These are starting proposals, not locked brand decisions.

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight navy | #06172D | Primary dark backgrounds, footer, navigation |
| Deep blue | #0B2545 | Secondary dark surfaces |
| Signal cyan | #098FB4 | Interactive accents |

| White | #FFFFFF | Cards and contrast surfaces |

Check contrast for every text/background combination.

## Typography and layout
Use a modern, legible sans-serif for body and interface elements. A restrained display face may be used for headings. Use clear hierarchy, comfortable line-height, short headings and readable content widths.

Use generous whitespace, consistent spacing, strong grid alignment, restrained borders and purposeful diagrams. Keep navigation simple. Design mobile layouts deliberately rather than shrinking desktop layouts.

## Imagery
Preferred: abstract illustrative market-data graphics, research/technology photography, subtle geometry, ecosystem diagrams and genuine approved team photography.

Avoid luxury cars, piles of cash, gold bars, rented-lifestyle imagery, fake dashboards, unverified performance charts, excessive red/green candlesticks, animated money counters and stock photos implying a team or office that does not exist.

## Motion
Use short, subtle transitions and hover/focus feedback. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Avoid scroll hijacking, parallax overload, flashing effects and distracting charts. Motion must not delay navigation or reduce readability.

## Voice
Professional, calm, confident, clear, internationally understandable and transparent about the company's stage.

Prefer “We are developing…”, “Our planned network…”, “Register your interest…” and “Explore a partnership…”.

Avoid guaranteed profits, “earn daily”, risk-free, get rich, unsubstantiated institutional-grade returns, or licensed/regulated claims unless verified for the specific entity and service.

## Components and accessibility
Build reusable header/navigation, mobile menu, footer/legal links, hero sections, business-line overview, audience CTA panels, enquiry forms/errors, insight cards, risk notice, buttons, links and section headings.

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, meaningful form labels/errors, text alternatives for informative graphics, reduced motion and status communication that does not rely on colour alone. Test narrow mobile widths and common desktop breakpoints.

## Design approval
Provide homepage direction, desktop/mobile previews, component/typography samples and a page-template preview. Explain how the design avoids unsupported claims and generic forex aesthetics. Obtain product-owner approval before building all pages.
