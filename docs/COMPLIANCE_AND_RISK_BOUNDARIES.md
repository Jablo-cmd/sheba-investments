# Sheba Investments — Compliance, Financial-Promotion and Risk Boundaries

## Purpose and status
This document gives developers product guardrails. It is not legal advice, a regulatory opinion or proof that Sheba holds authorisation.

Sheba is being planned in South Africa and may interact with users/providers elsewhere. Legal treatment depends on actual activity, instruments, audience, compensation, communication and jurisdiction. Obtain advice from appropriately qualified South African counsel and relevant specialists before launching financial services.

## Fundamental rule
A website disclaimer does not make an otherwise regulated activity unregulated. Calling a service education, research, signals, community, technology or introductions does not determine its legal classification. The substance of the activity matters.

No developer may activate a financial service, payment flow, broker referral programme, paid signals product or account-management feature solely on this document.

## Professional assessment required
Obtain written assessment covering, as applicable:
- FAIS implications.
- Whether recommendations, signals, referrals, introductions or intermediary activities require authorisation or an approved operating arrangement.
- FSCA requirements and applicable conduct standards.
- Financial promotions and advertising.
- Consumer protection and electronic communications.
- Privacy and personal-information obligations, including POPIA where applicable.
- AML, customer due diligence and recordkeeping where applicable.
- Cross-border marketing, client eligibility and provider restrictions.
- Tax, company and accounting treatment of rewards, commissions and investment income.

Assess the actual workflow, not just website wording.

## Proprietary trading controls
Before Sheba engages a trader to operate a prop-firm account:
1. Obtain current provider agreement and programme rules.
2. Confirm whether the account is simulated or live.
3. Confirm whether a company, employee, contractor or third party may trade it.
4. Confirm restrictions on credentials, account management, copying, IP/device use, automation, hedging, news trading and holding periods.
5. Confirm payout eligibility, identity, tax documentation and beneficiary rules.
6. Obtain written provider confirmation where terms are unclear.
7. Retain agreement and approval evidence.
8. Put trader engagement, confidentiality, IP, risk and reward terms in writing.

Do not build credential-sharing workflows. Do not grant access unless the provider permits the exact arrangement. Marketing language about a funded account is not proof Sheba owns notional capital or may delegate trading.

## Signals and research
Before external distribution, assess whether content constitutes advice, recommendation, intermediary service or another regulated activity; audience and jurisdiction; general versus personalised content; required authorisation, supervision, disclosures and records; conflicts including Sheba positions or broker remuneration; client protections; and performance methodology.

If approved, every signal must be timestamped and versioned. Record losses and cancellations as well as wins. Never backdate, delete or silently alter publications. No guaranteed-return language, certainty claims, risk-free statements or pressure tactics.

## Broker introductions
Before activating a referral arrangement, verify provider legal entity, permissions and client eligibility; review contract and permitted marketing; assess intermediary-service implications; confirm attribution and commission calculations; document conflicts and disclosures; confirm complaint routing; and avoid unsupported claims about spreads, execution, withdrawals, safety or regulation.

Do not display a broker as a partner until due diligence, agreement and publication approval are complete. Commercial incentives must not reward unnecessary trading.

## Client funds and custody
The initial website must not accept trading deposits, pool or hold user money, custody cryptocurrency/assets, process withdrawals, collect wallet seed phrases/private keys, request broker passwords or claim banking, custody or payment services.

Any future company treasury/wallet arrangement is a separate corporate finance project, not custody of customer assets.

## Website claims
Do not publish without evidence and owner approval: licensed/regulated/authorised claims; broker or prop-firm logos; trader/customer/account numbers; assets under management; profit figures, win rates, returns or verified track records; testimonials; claims Sheba owns provider capital; or claims of service availability in a jurisdiction.

Maintain a claims register with claim, evidence, owner, approval date, review date and approved channels. If unverified, omit or label as future ambition.

## Risk disclosure and content workflow
A risk disclosure should explain that leveraged products such as forex and CFDs can involve substantial losses, losses may occur quickly, and past performance does not guarantee future results. Tailor it to actual services and jurisdictions after legal review. A generic statement does not replace service-specific disclosures, client protections or authorisation.

For content about an instrument, trading opportunity, broker or investment: author drafts; classification is assessed; required reviewer/compliance approval is obtained; conflicts are disclosed where required; publication is timestamped/versioned; corrections are recorded transparently.

## Release gate
Before a regulated or potentially regulated service is activated, the product owner must provide written evidence of legal classification/advice, required authorisation or approved arrangement, provider contracts/permissions, approved disclosures/terms, operational owner and complaint process, privacy/security assessment, training/recordkeeping requirements and approved marketing claims.

Until then, keep the feature disabled and do not market it as available.
